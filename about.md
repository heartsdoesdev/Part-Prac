# Part Prac

**Love Studios** • Select a section. Practise it on repeat.

Shift-click Play, confirm **Enter Part Prac Mode**, and drag from your entry point to the section's end. Press Escape, then **Prac**.

Space + drag pans; +/- zooms. Quit practice to adjust your selection.

**Capture** plays the lead-in once and saves a native checkpoint at the section entry for route-dependent triggers. Regular **Prac** jumps directly to a native Start Pos; arbitrary earlier input-dependent effects require Capture.

Windows / classic forward sections only. Original levels stay unchanged. Initial build: in-game verification pending.
